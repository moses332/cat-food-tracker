Picky Paws

How to use it

← Back to the app

👋 What is this?

Picky Paws tracks what cat food Sybil likes and dislikes, so whoever's feeding her knows what to reach for. Every feeding gets logged with a rating, and the app keeps a leaderboard of her favourites.

🔑 Signing in

  1. Open the app and enter your email address (the one you were added with).
  2. Tap “Email me a code”.
  3. Check your email for a message from Picky Paws with a numeric code (check your spam folder if it's not there in a minute).
  4. Type the code in and tap Sign in. First time, it'll ask your name — that's what shows on feedings you log.

You'll stay signed in, so you normally only do this once. If a code doesn't arrive, wait a couple of minutes and tap to resend.

🍽️ Logging a feeding

  1. On the History screen, tap “＋ New feeding”.
  2. Pick the food — tap “📷 Scan a can” and point your camera at the barcode, or type a word or two in the search box (e.g. salmon pâté) and tap the match. Foods she's had lately are listed first.
  3. Tap how she reacted (see the ratings below).
  4. The time is set to now — change it if you're logging it later.
  5. Tap Save feeding. It'll pop into the history list.

😻 What the ratings mean

😻 Liked it (3) — ate it happily
😐 Nibbled (2) — ate some, left the rest
🙅 Refused (1) — walked away, ate nothing

Averages on the Insights tab use these numbers, out of 3.

📷 Scanning cans

The first time you scan a particular can, the app looks it up online and fills in the brand, product line and flavor for you — check them and tap Save & use. After that, scanning that can picks the food instantly. If a barcode won't scan, tap “Can't scan? Enter the number” and type the digits, or just search for the food instead.

✏️ Fixing a mistake

On any feeding in the History list, tap the pencil ✎ to change the rating, time, or notes, or the bin 🗑 to delete it. Everyone sees the same list, so your changes show up on the owners' phones too.

📊 What to open next

The Insights tab starts with Suggested next: foods she likes that she hasn't had in the last two days. Tap one to log it. Below that are her Favorites and the foods she's Most fed — switch between the last week, month, 3 months or all time, and tap “See all” for the full list.

🆘 Stuck?

If you can't sign in or something looks broken, message the owners — they can add or fix your access in under a minute, even remotely.
